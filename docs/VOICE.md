# Voice and tone

How LocalBuddy sounds wherever a member reads it: the app, the landing page, an email. The
landing page is the reference, because it already sounds right ("The bar with the password on
the door."). The app's screens should sound like the same person talking.

## In one line

A well-travelled friend who lives there: specific, warm, a little dry, never selling.

## Rules

1. **Name the concrete thing.** A bar, a market, an afternoon beats "experiences" or "content".
   If a line could sit in any app, rewrite it until it could only sit in this one.
2. **Short.** One idea per sentence, 20 words at most, 12 on a button. A fragment is fine when
   it has rhythm.
3. **Say "you".** "We" is LocalBuddy, and rare. Never "the user".
4. **Verbs first on actions, and say what happens for the person**, not what the system does:
   "Say hello", not "Send message".
5. **Money and safety stay plain.** Say what a payment buys and what it never buys: the time
   together is never paid for. Never promise earnings or perks. Reports, blocks, legal duties
   and errors get calm, exact words: no jokes.
6. **Errors say what happened and what to do next.** No "Oops", no blame, no jargon. Say
   "check your connection" only when the connection really is what failed.
7. **Warm is not gushing.** No exclamation marks (one is allowed on the match screen). No
   "amazing", "unique", "journey", "platform", "users", no em dashes.
8. **Empty states are a chance to be human:** say what will fill the space and what to do now.
9. **Members are equals.** Travellers are people like you, hosts are people with an afternoon.
   Never "providers" or "listings".
10. **Humour only where nothing is at stake** (taglines, empty states, onboarding). Dry, never
    at a person's expense.

## What the reference sites teach (patterns, not phrases: do not copy their lines)

- **Couchsurfing:** short beats and a rhythm of threes, direct "you", hosts as warm and trusted,
  travellers as peers, members named by first name and city. Free hospitality is the point.
- **Airbnb Experiences:** a vivid verb opens the line, the place is named down to the
  neighbourhood, the person is the selling point, the invitation to host is one easy sentence.
  We borrow the specificity, not the money: nobody here is paid for their time.

## Fixed words (stable in every language)

host, traveller, chat, introduction (the one thing a payment buys), verified, "both said yes"
for a match.

## Languages

- English: UK spelling.
- Italian: informal "tu". Product words stay: host, ospitare, mostrare interesse, match, chat.
- Spanish (Spain): informal "tú".
- French: "vous" throughout; no-break space before `: ? ! ;`.

Translate the intent and the rhythm, not the words. If a literal translation is flat, write the
line again in that language.
